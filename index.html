Human control first

Early outbound messages are drafts for Bianca to approve. Sensitive, ambiguous, or compliance-related items go to a named owner.

02

Old Mutual browser pilot

A browser operator may inspect cases and assist with routine steps. Reliability, access rules, and page changes require iterative testing; production automation is not a phase-one dependency.

Jev decision model

Jev classifies and recommends routes. The on-site server applies explicit rules, tracks cases, and records approvals. ChatGPT or Claude subscriptions remain optional staff tools.

DELIVERY

One month focused on Bianca.

Build the reusable foundation while making the first mailbox useful. The exact acceptance list is agreed after sample emails, tracker data, and follow-up rules arrive.

WEEK 01

Connect and learn

Secure Graph access, import a sample tracker, label real email examples, confirm owners and case IDs.

WEEK 02

Classify and queue

Monitor Bianca’s mailbox, classify work, match cases, and send low-confidence items for review.

WEEK 03

Trigger and follow up

Quote clock, section 14 weekly queue, new business daily queue, and servicing SLA drafts.

WEEK 04

Prove and hand over

Test against real examples, refine rules with Bianca, train staff, and agree which actions may be automated.

Phase one target

Bianca’s intake, routing, case register, approval queue, and the highest-value follow-up paths.

Investigate alongside

Old Mutual browser interaction, PDF extraction, and expansion to shared mailboxes.

Later expansion

Campaigns, WhatsApp intake, wider team roll-out, and richer reports after the core paths work.
